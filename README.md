# 🚗 Used Car Sales Analysis and Business Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing historical used car auction data to identify sales trends, manufacturer performance, vehicle pricing patterns, and other business insights using Python.

The analysis uses data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), historical USD-to-INR conversion, and data visualisation to understand the used car auction market.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse monthly used car sales trends.
- Identify top-performing car manufacturers.
- Compare average selling prices across brands.
- Analyse selling prices against MMR (Manheim Market Report).
- Understand sales distribution by vehicle age.
- Explore vehicle condition and transmission types.
- Identify useful insights to support inventory and pricing decisions.

## 📊 Data Analysis and Visualisations

The project includes the following visualisations:

1. Monthly Vehicle Sales – Analyses sales volume across months.
2. Top 10 Manufacturers – Identifies manufacturers with the highest auction sales.
3. Top 10 Brands by Average Selling Price – Compares average selling prices across selected brands.
4. Selling Price vs MMR – Compares actual selling prices with the MMR reference value.
5. Vehicle Sales by Age Group – Examines the distribution of vehicles by age.
6. Vehicle Condition Distribution – Explores the distribution of vehicle condition ratings.
7. Transmission Type Distribution – Compares automatic, manual, and unknown transmission categories.

## 💡 Business Insights

This analysis helps understand:

- Monthly auction sales patterns.
- Manufacturer performance by sales volume.
- Differences in average selling prices between brands.
- How auction selling prices compare with market reference values.
- Vehicle age and condition distributions.
- Transmission category distribution.

These insights can support vehicle evaluation, inventory planning, and auction pricing decisions.

## 📁 Dataset Information

- Dataset: Used Car Auction Sales
- Original records: 558,837
- Original columns: 16
- Data period: December 2013 – July 2015
- Data source: Historical used car auction dataset.

**Note:** This is historical US auction data. USD-to-INR conversions use historical exchange rates and do not represent current Indian used car market prices.
